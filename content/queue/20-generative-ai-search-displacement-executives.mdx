## How Large Language Models Evaluate Executive Credibility

Generative AI engines do not rely on traditional keyword density. Instead, they evaluate digital authority through three algorithmic layers:

1. **Entity Citation Density:** How frequently your full name is co-cited alongside specific industry ontologies (e.g., "AI governance," "enterprise supply chain resilience") across accredited digital publications.
2. **Triangulation of Truth:** Whether factual assertions on your personal website are corroborated by independent third-party sources like Wikipedia, SEC filings, and peer-reviewed journals.
3. **Structured Information Architecture:** How easily AI crawlers can ingest your biographical milestones, published whitepapers, and corporate directorships via schema markup.
