## Critical Mistakes to Avoid When Hiring a Branding Consultant

The most common error is hiring an influencer marketing coach instead of a dedicated corporate advisor. Social media coaches focus on vanity follower metrics that hold zero value in boardrooms.

Another pitfall is engaging a traditional PR firm that charges exorbitant monthly retainers without building owned digital infrastructure. PR placements fade quickly; owned websites compound indefinitely.

Furthermore, beware of consultants who demand that you draft your own content. A true executive partner extracts insights through dialogue and manages all writing.

**Actionable Takeaway:** Screen potential advisors specifically on their understanding of corporate governance and executive recruiting dynamics.
