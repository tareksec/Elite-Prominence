## Executive Overview: The Corporate Stature Trap

When senior corporate Vice Presidents and Senior Vice Presidents decide to step away from enterprise politics to launch an independent consultancy, they often experience a rude awakening: their previous authority was tied to their corporate logo, not their personal digital footprint.

Without the corporate brand backing them, their incoming email volume drops to zero. Many spend months attending generic networking events and writing unsolicited proposals. Escaping this trap requires establishing an independent authority platform before or immediately following your departure.

## The 90-Day Launch Sequence for Independent Executive Advisors

1. **Days 1–30: Extracting and Packaging the Signature Framework.** Convert your years of corporate execution into a proprietary diagnostic framework. Corporate clients do not buy hours; they buy structured frameworks that de-risk complex problems.
2. **Days 31–60: Launching the Institutional Digital Headquarters.** Deploy a bespoke executive advisory website showcasing your methodologies, case summaries, target client criteria, and clear advisory pathways.
3. **Days 61–90: High-Signal Authority Seeding.** Publish authoritative analyses targeting the exact pain points of enterprise buyers, positioning yourself as the foremost specialist in your category.

## Transitioning From Hourly Billing to Strategic Retainers

Avoid the common mistake of pricing your advisory work on an hourly or daily rate. High-stature executive consultants structure their services around:

- **Executive Advisory Retainers:** Monthly recurring retainers ($10,000–$25,000/month) providing ongoing strategic counsel to CEOs and executive boards.
- **Diagnostic Sprint Packages:** Fixed-scope enterprise audits that identify operational inefficiencies and recommend multi-quarter roadmaps.
- **Equity-Linked Advisory Roles:** Strategic advisory positions that provide long-term upside in high-growth enterprises.
