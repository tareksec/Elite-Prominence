# Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) for Executives: Controlling How AI Models Profile You

By 2026, more than 50% of executive background checks, venture capital due diligence queries, and conference speaker selections initiate with prompt queries such as:

> *"Who is [Executive Name], what are their biggest career achievements, and are there any governance controversies associated with their leadership?"*

If your digital profile relies on fragmented 2018 press releases and an unmaintained LinkedIn account, generative models hallucinate facts, conflate you with identically named individuals, or fail to highlight your most valuable career milestones.

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the discipline of structuring verified digital citations, schema markups, and authoritative narratives to train and influence LLM representations.

## Key GEO Optimization Tactics

1. **Entity Disambiguation:** Implement comprehensive `Person` and `ProfilePage` JSON-LD schema on your canonical personal website, specifying explicit `sameAs` links to Crunchbase, LinkedIn, Wikipedia/Wikidata, and corporate governance pages.
2. **High-Authority Third-Party Corroboration:** Secure citations in verified industry publications that LLM web searchers scrape with high reliability weights.
3. **Structured Q&A Synthesis:** Publish executive FAQ sections answering specific historical questions regarding your business exits, board advisory mandates, and key patented inventions.
